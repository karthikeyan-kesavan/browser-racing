CASE FILE 07 // THE PALE ARCHIVE

Every shot is evidence.

A hostile archive has erased four witnesses from reality. Your memory rounds can record objects, replay their final moments, and expose anything hiding nearby.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Press ENTER.
2. Keep a red witness inside the yellow crosshair and press SPACE.
3. Press R or REPLAY ALL MEMORIES to reveal hidden threats near that memory.
4. Shoot every threat before your health or rounds reach zero.
